# Skeletal Mechanobiology & Biomechanics Lab
Welcome to the GitHub of the [Skeletal Mechanobiology & Biomechanics Lab](https://morganresearchlab.org/) at Boston University. 
We study the interplay between bone and mechanics, and sometimes develop software tools to further that pursuit!

For more information, please see our [website](https://morganresearchlab.org/) or review our [publications](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hLf0lzEAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao).
